172 ESTIMATING AND COSTING

LINE PLAN OF BUILDING

In case of line plan of a building, the dimensions of internal measurements of the rooms are
usually given in the plan. In such cases before undertaking the work of estimating, the detailed plan
of the building showing the walls, doors, windows, etc., should be drawn and then cross-section of
the building with usual foundations and footings shquld be drawn showing all dimensions. After
drawing the plan and section, general specifications may be assumed suitably and then the estimate
may be prepared in the usual manner.

If quick work is required without drawing the detailed plan and the cross-section of the
building the total centre line length of all walls may be found by assuming the thickness of walls and
then the quantities of different items of work may be calculated with this total centre line length by
multiplying the respective breadth and or height. Breadth and height may be suitably asumed or’
a cross-section of wall may be drawn or imagined.

If an approximate estimate is required then the dimensions of the lines in the line plan may be
considered as those of the centre line of walls and the total centre line length may be found
approximately from these dimensions and then the quantities of different items of works may be
calculated approximately with this total centre line length by multiplying by the assumed breadth
and height of the respective items.
