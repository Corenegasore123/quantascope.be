CHAPTER 3
ESTIMATE OF BUILDINGS

Detailed estimates of various types of buildings and works, starting from simple one have been
dealt in this chapter. Both the methods, the General Method (Long wall and short wall method) and
the Centre line method, have been adopted to illustrate the application of these methods. For
clarification of the dimensions a column, Explanatory notes, has been added so that the beginners
may not find any difficulty to understand. If the beginners go through these estimates, they will be
able to prepare estimate of any type of building.
