MIXED MASONRY 155

Sometimes walls of a building are constructed with different kinds of mortar, certain portion
with rich mortar and certain other portion with weak mortar. The jambs of doors and windows and
small pillars in between them, and arches may need rich mortar, and the remaining portions of the
walls may be of weak mortar. Some poftions may also be of stone masonry and some portions of
brick masonry. For economy sometimes the wails are also constructed with mud mortar except the
jambs and sills of doors and windows, the upper one or two layers below roof and lower one or two
layers above plinth, arches and verandah pillars which are made with cement mortar or lime
mortar. In such cases for estimating, the masonry which forms the mass of the wall is calculated as

Solid, and the masonry in jambs, sills, upper and lower layers, arches, pillars, etc., are calculated.
Then to get the quantity of mass masonry deductions are made from solid masonry for :—
@ Masonry in jambs, sills upper and lower layers, etc.,
Gi) Masonry in arches or lintels,
(iii) Openings.
